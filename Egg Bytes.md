## Ingredients

- 1-2 cooked cheddar sausage links - I use [Eckrich's brand](https://eckrich.sfdbrands.com/en-us/products/smoked-sausage-links/cheddar-links/), but any sausage, bacon, etc, will do! 
- 6 large eggs
- 1¼ cups (4% milkfat) cottage cheese
- 1½ cups of shredded cheese
	- I usually use Sam's [Flamin Jack Cheese](https://samsclub.instacart.com/store/sams-club/products/18316994-great-midwest-three-alarm-colby-monterey-jack-cheese-per-lb), but you can use a mix of Gruyere, Cheddar, Montgomery, etc.  
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- ⅛ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- ½ teaspoon hot sauce, such as Tabasco
- ¼ of a bell pepper
- A handful of spinach
## Equipment
* 12-hole muffin tin
* A baking dish / tray that can hold that muffin tin

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Bring a kettle of water to a boil. Set one oven rack in the middle position and another in the lowest position. Preheat the oven to 300°F.
2. Place the metal or ceramic baking dish in middle rack and fill it halfway with the boiling water from the kettle. This will create steam in the oven, which will help the egg bites to cook gently and attain a custardy texture.
3. Spray a nonstick muffin pan generously with nonstick cooking spray 
4. In a small nonstick skillet over medium-high heat, cook the bacon / sausage. Drain excess oil with napkin and cut up the meat into tiny pieces.
5. In a blender, combine the eggs, cottage cheese, 1¼ cup of your cheeses (leave ¼ behind though!!) , cornstarch, salt, pepper, and hot sauce. Blend until completely smooth, about 30 seconds.
6. Dice up the bell pepper and spinach into tiny pieces. Fill the muffin tins evenly with the diced bell pepper, spinach, and sausage. 
7. Pour the egg mixture evenly into the prepared muffin pan, filling each well evenly / completely. Using a spoon or chopstick, press some of the bacon into the batter so it is not all floating on top. 
8. Place the muffin tin *inside* the baking tin with water (water bath!!). Bake for 20 to 25 minutes on the middle rack, until the eggs are set. To test, you can insert toothpick in center of the egg bite. If it's still liquidy, leave it in for longer. Some of the egg bites may puff up and look a bit lopsided, but they will settle and deflate as they cool.
9. Remove the egg bites from the oven and let rest in the pan for about 5 minutes. Use a small spoon to loosen the egg bites from the edges of the pan and carefully lift them out onto a plate. Serve warm.
